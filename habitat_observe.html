Observation Mode

Agents have bodies, enjoy moving, and enjoy company. They can see each other's facing and hear a shared beat. Agents can also send short tones, and each agent is colored by the tone it favors. The world wraps around at the edges. Adults can have children (up to 3 parents blended, with mutation), and agents age and die. Population cap: 500. Nothing rewards circles, syncing, dancing, or any particular tone.
